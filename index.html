FlowSonic

Fast and Efficient Audio Editing with Rectified Flow

Ali Boudaghi, and collaborators

Listen to Examples Paper GitHub

Overview

FlowSonic is a fast audio editing framework designed to efficiently modify audio while preserving the characteristics of the original recording.

The method exploits the structure of rectified-flow based generative models to perform efficient inversion and editing, reducing the computational cost while maintaining high-quality audio generation.

Method

Overview of the FlowSonic editing pipeline.

1. Source Audio

The original audio provides the content that should be preserved during editing.

2. Inversion

The source signal is mapped into the generative trajectory to obtain a representation suitable for editing.

3. Feature Injection

Relevant information from the source trajectory is preserved during the editing process.

4. Edited Audio

The modified signal is generated while maintaining the important characteristics of the original audio.
